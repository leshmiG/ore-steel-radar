# Ore & Steel Radar brief, 2026-11-02

96 headlines scanned, 24 relevant signals (2026-10-27 to 2026-11-02).

## CEO
- **[8/10 tailwind] LKAB is shutting one of its Kiruna pellet plants for several months, which cuts its 2026 pellet shipments.** Less pellet supply from a major European seller. Its DR and BF pellet customers will look for other suppliers, which supports premiums. *Action:* Contact LKAB's European customers this month with spot and term offers. ([Mysteel](https://www.mysteel.net/news/5118092-lkab-to-shut-kiruna-pellet-plant-for-months-cutting-2026-shipments))
- **[8/10 headwind] Jindal Steel Duqm has procured a second plant for its steel complex in Duqm, Oman.** A GCC neighbour is adding capacity close to our markets. Depending on the plant type, it either competes for our customers or becomes a pellet buyer. *Action:* Confirm what the second plant is and its start date; brief the CEO this week. ([Oman Observer](https://www.omanobserver.om/article/1176702/business/jindal-steel-duqm-procures-second-plant-for-oman-complex))
- **[7/10 neutral] Vale's Q3 2026 iron ore output fell 6% year on year to 88.4 Mt and net profit dropped 53%; full-year output is guided to the low end of its range.** Less supply from the largest seaborne producer can firm premiums for high-grade ore and pellet feed. Vale's pellet volumes decide how much of this reaches our pricing. *Action:* Pull Vale's 3Q26 report for pellet volumes; update the Q1 pricing view. ([IndexBox](https://www.indexbox.io/blog/vale-reports-6-drop-in-q3-2026-iron-ore-output-to-884-million-tonnes/))
- **[7/10 neutral] Shipments from the Simandou iron ore project in Guinea could exceed 20 Mt in 2026.** A large new high-grade supply source pressures iron ore prices: cheaper feed for us, but it can also weigh on pellet prices. *Action:* Add Simandou volumes to the 2027 ore price and feed cost scenarios. ([GMK Center](https://gmk.center/en/news/ore-shipments-from-simandou-could-exceed-20-million-tonnes-in-2026/))
- **[6/10 tailwind] U.S. Steel broke ground on a $1.9 billion direct reduced iron plant in Osceola, Arkansas.** Another large DRI plant adds global demand for DR-grade pellets. It will likely use US pellets, but it tightens the DR-grade market overall and supports premiums. *Action:* Add to the DR-grade demand model; track how it sources pellets. ([Shanghai Metals Market](https://news.metal.com/newscontent/104189804-us-steel-breaks-ground-on-19-billion-dri-plant-in-osceola-arkansas))
